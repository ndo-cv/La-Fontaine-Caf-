Our Story

Where the neighbourhood
gathers, morning to afternoon.

La Fontaine was born from a simple idea: that great coffee and honest food, served in a welcoming space, can make an ordinary morning feel like something worth savouring. We opened our doors on Onslow Parade because we believed Southgate deserved a café that felt like it truly belonged here.

Every morning we grind fresh beans, bake our own specials and lay out a menu that spans proper breakfasts, handcrafted eggs and long, lazy lunches. Whether you are stopping in for a quick espresso before the tube, settling in for a full English with the newspaper, or treating yourself to avocado toast and a turmeric latte — you will always find a familiar face and a warm welcome.

We are proudly child-friendly, community-focused and committed to catering for everyone — with generous vegetarian and vegan options alongside our classic favourites. This is your café, your corner of N14.

— The La Fontaine Team
